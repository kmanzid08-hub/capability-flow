# Data model

All primary keys are UUIDs and timestamps are timezone-aware UTC values generated by the database.

- **Organization**: tenant workspace with unique slug and lifecycle status.
- **User**: global authentication identity with unique normalized email.
- **OrganizationMembership**: user-to-organization association and role. `(organization_id, user_id)` is unique.
- **Person**: organization-owned resource profile. It carries `organization_id`, creator/updater user IDs, availability, and profile lifecycle status.

Membership roles are `owner`, `admin`, `manager`, `data_entry`, `reviewer`, and `viewer`. Owners, admins, managers, and data-entry members may mutate Person records; all active members may read them. Archived people are excluded from normal reads.

Future qualifications, skills, experience, evidence, opportunities, and requirements must carry `organization_id` even when tenant ownership could be inferred through a parent. This supports direct policy enforcement and auditing.
